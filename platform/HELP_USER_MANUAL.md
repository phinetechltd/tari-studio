# Agency Platform – User Manual & Help

## Overview
Multi-tenant AI marketing, sales & support platform for agencies. Make AI video and image ads, publish to social, automate WhatsApp replies, track leads and run campaigns.

## Getting Started

### Sign up
Go to `/signup`. Enter work email, create password. Confirm email via link sent. New accounts start with setup wizard.

### Verify email
Click verification link. Use `/verify` to resend.

### Accept invitation
Open `/accept-invite/[token]`, set password, join organisation.

### Setup wizard
First login redirects to `/setup`. Complete:
1. Brand details
2. Connect social channels
3. Payment method
Progress saved.

### Login & MFA
Sign in at `/login`. Roles with publishing require MFA in `/security`.

## Account & Billing

### Profile & Security
Edit name/email/password in `/account`. MFA and sessions in `/security`.

### Organisations
Owners manage members, roles, permissions in `/platform/orgs`. Switch orgs from top bar.

### Plans & Credits
Choose plan in `/platform/subscriptions`. Credits buy video/image generations. Credits never expire.

### Payments
Pay by M-Pesa or card in `/platform/payments`. View invoices.

## Create Content

### Studio – Video ads
`/content`. Prompt, brand, aspect ratio, length. Credit cost shown before generation. Up to 10s per step with sound.

### Studio – Image ads
Select Image in Studio. Poster/product/square. Cost per plan.

### Library
`/content/assets`. Filter by type, brand, status. Download/archive.

### Brands
`/app/brands`. Logo, slogan, colours. Used for consistent captions and Autopilot.

### Templates
`/app/templates`. Reusable prompts.

### Characters
`/app/characters`. Spokespeople/mascots.

## Grow & Distribute

### Campaigns
`/app/campaigns`. Tracked links with ref codes.

### Social publishing
`/app/social`. Connect Facebook/Instagram. Schedule posts.

### Inbox & WhatsApp
`/app/inbox`. Replies. Automations in `/app/automations` auto-reply.

### Leads
`/app/leads`. Contacts from links/WhatsApp.

### Autopilot
`/app/autopilot`. Recurring posts, approve before publish.

## Business Operations

### Products & Catalogue
`/app/products`. For WhatsApp replies and orders.

### Orders
`/app/orders`. Done-for-you jobs.

### Teams
`/app/team`. Invite members, assign roles.

## AI Helper
Platform assistant is available at `/app/assistant`. Uses default system AI from admin config. Ask about brands, characters, templates, prompts.

## Search Help
Visit `/help` for searchable manual with sections and live search.

## Support
Email support via `SUPPORT_EMAIL`. Use AI Helper for instant guidance.
